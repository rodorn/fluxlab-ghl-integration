# Custom GHL API integrations & automations for agencies, I build, you brand it

## EN

**I'm the developer behind your GoHighLevel integrations, you keep the client relationship, I stay invisible.**

Agencies win the client, own the strategy, and run the account. But sooner or later a client needs something GHL can't do out of the box: a webhook into their CRM, a two-way sync with an external tool, a data migration, or a broken automation that has to work _today_. That's where I come in, as your silent, white-label technical partner.

### What I build

- **Custom API integrations**, connect GHL to anything with an API: CRMs, billing/invoicing, calendars, e-commerce, internal databases, or custom dashboards. REST, OAuth, service accounts, handled.
- **Webhook flows**, reliable inbound/outbound webhooks with validation, normalization, retries and logging (no more "the lead didn't come through"). The proof piece in this repo is exactly this: a GHL lead webhook that lands in a Google Sheet and forwards to an external API.
- **Migrations & snapshots**, move sub-accounts, rebuild or port snapshots, bulk-migrate contacts/pipelines/custom fields between locations or from another platform.
- **Fixing broken automations**, diagnose and repair workflows, Zapier/Make scenarios and half-finished integrations that another dev left behind.
- **Data plumbing**, scheduled syncs, ETL, reporting exports, deduplication, enrichment.

### How I work

- **White-label by default.** Everything ships under your brand. I don't contact your clients; I don't put my name on anything client-facing.
- **Async, written-first communication.** Scope, updates and handover happen in writing (Slack, email, Loom, docs). No standing calls required, you get clear written progress you can forward straight to the client. Calls only when genuinely needed.
- **Small, verifiable steps.** You get working increments and a short write-up of what was built and how it was tested, not a black box.
- **Clean handover.** Documented code, env-based config, a README, and a walkthrough so your team can maintain it (or keep me on retainer, your call).

### Rates

- **Hourly:** $25–75/h depending on scope and urgency.
- **Fixed-price projects:** $500–3,000 for a defined integration or automation.
- **Retainer:** available for agencies that need ongoing dev capacity, monthly block of hours at a discounted rate.

_Typical starters: a single webhook flow (~$500–900), a two-way API sync (~$1,200–2,500), a snapshot migration ($800–2,000)._

### Why me

Python developer focused on APIs and automation. I write resilient integrations (validation, retries, logging), the boring-but-critical parts that keep an agency's automations from silently failing. Working proof-of-concept available on request; sample code in this repository.

**Next step:** send me the integration you need in a sentence or two and the two systems involved. I'll reply in writing with scope, a fixed price or hour estimate, and a timeline.
